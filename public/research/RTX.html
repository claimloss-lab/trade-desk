    • 10. Inversion / Anti-thesis

  • If backlog is large but cannot be converted because of suppliers, labor or quality constraints, it is not equivalent to near-term cash flow; bear floor in this model is $125 (Judgment, 2026-09-20)
  • If GTF remediation becomes a recurring engineering/safety event, Pratt can consume cash while damaging customer trust; this is the most company-specific permanent-loss path to monitor (source basis: installed-base/GTF discussion in sources/RTX/transcript.md, 2026-09-20)
  • If price embeds a high multiple while earnings are still dependent on normalization, even a good business can produce weak shareholder returns; peer and historical multiples remain missing (source limitation, 2026-09-20)

    • 11. Portfolio Strategy

  • Treat RTX as a quality-but-cyclical aerospace/defense exposure; track organic sales, segment margin, FCF conversion, backlog burn, book-to-bill and GTF cost separately (Judgment, 2026-09-20)
  • DCA/entry/position cap not specified as a recommendation. A discipline rule is not to size from backlog alone and not to add when thesis requires Bull assumptions for normal returns (Judgment, 2026-09-20)
  • Exit/kill: new material GTF safety/inspection event, persistent inability to convert backlog into FCF, structural defense budget cancellation, or commercial aftermarket/production deterioration without offset from Raytheon (source basis: transcript + SEC facts, 2026-09-20)
